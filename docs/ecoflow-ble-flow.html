How the app pairs over BLE, does an ECDH handshake, and decrypts the stream.

No EcoFlow servers are involved. The host radio scans, connects to the station's GATT service, negotiates an AES‑128 session key over SECP160r1, authenticates with MD5(userId + sn), and starts decoding encrypted 0x5A5A frames in place.
